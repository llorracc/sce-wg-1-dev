Buffer Stock Theory · the liquidity-constrained solution as a limit

As the zero-income risk vanishes, precaution turns into the liquidity constraint

Four households with BST's calibration. Three face a natural borrowing constraint, kept in force by a small probability ℘ of a quarter with no income at all; they never borrow, because a zero-income draw could leave them with nothing. The fourth, the restrained consumer, has no zero-income risk and simply may not borrow: c ≤ m. As ℘ falls, the first three rules close in on the fourth. Two toolkits solved the four households independently, HARK and dolo, on the same shock nodes and savings grids: their rules coincide.
