When Matsya is consulted about a design question that bears on these schemas, its answer is weighed against what the
schema project needs, and where it does not fit, the disagreement is stated with its reason rather than the answer passed
through. The owner's instruction of 2026-10-18, given while Matsya was being asked for the right fix to Bellman's
parameter-condition defect.

**Why:** Matsya answers in Bellman's terms, from the material it is given; the schemas have needs of their own, the stage
file as a checked statement of the model, one written form of a member, the checker owning the comparisons the language
cannot decide, and comparability across toolkits, which an answer that is right for Bellman alone may not serve.

**How to apply:** read each answer against those needs; where it fits, say so and act; where it does not, say what does not
fit and why, and offer the further question that would settle it, which the owner may send.
