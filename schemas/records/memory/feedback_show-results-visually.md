When a result's point is a shape, such as rules converging, a kink smoothed away or a gap shrinking, show it to the owner
as a picture: a published page with the figures, built from the results files, beside the tables.

**Why:** on 2026-11-01 (18:22:40) the owner, given the restrained-limit suite's tables, asked for an artifact showing the
four rules, since numerical results are not nearly as striking as the visual picture. The page "The Restrained Limit"
answered it.

**How to apply:** build the page from the recorded results files with a script, keep the script and the page's source
in the findings record, and say in a caption whatever the picture computes rather than reads. Related:
[[feedback_say-what-tests-establish]].
