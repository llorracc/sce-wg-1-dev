# Rulings of 2026-11-04 on the project's purpose

The owner's decisions, in substance and by time (EST). His words are kept outside this public repository. The
reference-solution rulings of the same day are in [their own file](2026-11-04_reference-solution.md).

- **Before 13:10:52, HAFiscal as a third toolkit set aside; the approach assessed.** The owner backs off the idea of
  HAFiscal as an example third toolkit, which the plan sketched in the reply before 11:57:54 had taken up, and steps
  back to assess the whole approach. He asks what the session perceives the purpose of the sce-wg-1 project to be, so
  that its advice on reaching the top-level goal can be judged against its understanding of it. The plan of that
  sketch stands aside; nothing of it is built.
- **Between 13:10:52 and 13:21:42, the project is its own test of the thesis.** The session's answer stated the purpose
  in layers (the working group's charge, Project A's thesis, the owner's charge for the fork, the apparatus mapped
  onto the proposal's five research themes) and gave most weight to one reading: that the project tests Project A's
  thesis on itself, since its adapters were written by an AI from the schemas and the toolkits' documentation alone,
  and the harness checks whether what they compute converges to the stated model's solution. The owner confirms that
  reading in full. What it settles: the apparatus is evidence that the definition is complete, not a benchmark of
  toolkits, and the measure of the schemas is whether an implementation written from them alone can be checked
  against them. What it leaves open: the near deliverable (the upstream pull requests, a paper, or a working tool),
  whether the AI-written-from-the-schemas reading is to be documented as evidence, what toy-hafiscal is for beyond
  carrying a published paper's model and estimation, and whether the other members' toolkits are expected to write
  stones for the buffer-stock families.
- **In the same stretch, a proposal requested: exact definitions of canonical models.** The owner asks for a proposal to
  amend the documentation of the working group's purpose so that, for the models the project encompasses, the group
  canonicalizes an exact definition of each, exact in the sense that a valid solution method is one that, given
  unbounded computation, converges to the same solution as every other valid solution method given unbounded
  computation. The proposal is [`project-a/exact-definition.md`](../../../project-a/exact-definition.md), PROPOSED:
  the criterion stated as definitions, the wording for each document, and the consequences. Nothing in the documents
  is changed.
- **Between 13:21:42 and 13:34:41, the proposal approved, as a standalone pull request.** The owner approves the proposal,
  with its change to his formulation (validity defined by honoring the definition with refinable choices, exactness as
  the shared limit of every valid method), and asks that it be a pull request of its own. Applied as
  [econ-ark/sce-wg-1 #14](https://github.com/econ-ark/sce-wg-1/pull/14) from the fork's branch `exact-definition`, the
  three working-group documents; the fork's own documents aligned in the working tree. What stays open: the co-author's
  reading of the special-issue proposal's wording, Project B's optional sentence, and the fork's landing page, behind
  upstream's, to be brought current when the pull request merges.
