# Ontologies

The ontologies of the working group. Each lives in a folder of its own, with its README, its version, its changelog and
its license, and is released on its own.

| folder | what it defines |
|---|---|
| [`probability-distributions/`](probability-distributions/) | the probability distributions the schemas and toolkits write: families, charts and the conversions between them, the operators that build laws from laws, and the rule deciding when two written laws are the same law |
